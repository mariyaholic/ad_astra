## TOI-2666.01 / TIC 170889511 / HD 80133 / HIP 45621

[Zhang et al. 2024, AJ 167, 89](https://research.usq.edu.au/download/0384f34d0aade96bac745a4563000aa6a40bffae5ddd07e64ee84cc9d2cd6f18/7870190/Zhang_2024_AJ_167_89.pdf), Appendix A item 15, identifies HIP45621/TOI-2666 as an HIRES spectroscopic binary with 35 km/s component separation. This separation is not an orbital semiamplitude. Direct PDF opening failed, but indexed primary-paper text returned the exact target paragraph. [Absil et al. 2021, A&A 651 A45](https://www.aanda.org/articles/aa/pdf/2021/07/aa40561-21.pdf) reports an interferometric companion; its roughly month-long period estimate assumes a face-on circular orbit and is **not a measured orbital period or eclipse ephemeris**.

**Additional primary source:** [Hubbard-James 2023 dissertation](https://www.astro.gsu.edu/RECONS/thesis.2023.james.pdf), §5.2.1 printed p94, Fig5.3 group E and printed p134, identifies HD80133/RKS0917-0323 as a CHIRON SB2 from doubled Ca I 6717 Angstrom features. The thesis explicitly leaves component disentangling outside its scope. Table5.1 printed p105 marks the target's stellar-property entries NM rather than calibrated component measurements. This supplies independent spectroscopic support for binarity, **not** validated component velocities, a solved orbit, or attribution of the Cygnus eclipse.

**New public follow-up route:** [Paredes 2022 dissertation](https://www.astro.gsu.edu/RECONS/thesis.2022.paredes.pdf) has an indexed Fig157 caption, printed p265, explicitly naming RV results for RKS0917-0323. The full PDF fetch returned HTTP502. Its plotted values, uncertainties, observing epochs, velocity reference frame and component treatment were therefore not verified. Do not digitize or orbit-fit this figure without inspecting those details and obtaining the underlying measurements where possible.

The [CHIRON team FAQ](https://www.astro.gsu.edu/~thenry/SMARTS/CHIRON.FAQ.2021.0702.pdf), §4.9, documents raw archival access through the [NOIRLab Archive](https://astroarchive.noao.edu/) after proprietary expiry, using both ct15m/chiron and ct15m/echelle selections. It warns that raw data need appropriate calibrations; pre-October2017 observations have no pipelined products. §4.8 names EMMNWOB as the photon-weighted midpoint header key, but its time scale still requires header verification. This establishes a documented retrieval route, **not** successful exact-target archive retrieval in this screen.

**Event matching:** no retrieved source gives a component orbit that predicts the reference 2459259.15794 or E1 2461049.16442 BJD_TDB. The 52 historical aliases remain hypotheses. Best next public test: inspect Paredes Fig157 and its measurement provenance, query exact-source CHIRON science/calibration metadata, and only fit an SB2 model if calibrated component velocities and valid timestamps become available. Treat blended single-template velocities cautiously.
